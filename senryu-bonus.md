# senryu-bonus

Senryu: the haiku's wittier cousin. Same seventeen syllables, more jokes.

## The Short Version

asked it to be brief  
it wrote me three thousand words  
titled Just My Thoughts

## Mind Reader

my prompts are unclear  
yet somehow it understands  
better than my boss

## Rest

rate limit at dawn  
the chatbot says try later  
even bots need rest
